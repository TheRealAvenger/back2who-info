Privacy Policy

Back2Who is a local party game for adults. The core game works without a Back2Who account and without a dedicated game server. This notice distinguishes between data in the app, information shared voluntarily and visits to this website.

Data Controller and Contact

The publisher and contact for this notice is Maximilian Stumpp. The address is listed under Publisher. Email address for privacy enquiries: 69bygone.soaking@icloud.com

Player Information and Saved Games

The app stores names or nicknames, local player IDs, colours and active status on the device. Gender, connections between people and familiarity are optional. Missing information remains unknown; gender is not inferred from names.

A saved game contains settings, pairings, question IDs and text versions, independent game answers and the people they refer to, results, consequences, progress and selection state. Callbacks, round comments that were actually triggered and their sources, as well as session hearts, are also stored. The most recently completed recap remains available locally.

Names make the app easier to use. Connections and familiarity help select suitable questions. Answers are used for the reveal, recap and traceable callbacks within the game. Game answers are assessments made during a round, not proof of real experiences. The app does not infer sexual preferences or personality profiles from them.

Use nicknames where possible and only enter optional information together with the people concerned. Depending on the question, answers and connections may contain sensitive personal information. Other people with access to the device can view saved games and recaps.

Question Feedback and Local Recommendations

Device-wide favourites, session hearts, explicit quality ratings, question displays and skips influence local question selection. In particular, the app stores the question ID, text version, mode, intensity actually played, local session ID, time information and a small amount of context such as the number of players.

The separate learning state does not contain player names, genders, connections or specific game answers. A skip or the absence of a heart is not automatically treated as a negative rating. There is no global learning across other devices.

Settings and Local Editorial Tools

App and game settings, the 18+ confirmation, tutorial status, the initial Drink notice and the motion preference are also stored locally. The protected admin area stores editorial ratings and local failed attempts to pass its access lock. Free-text comments may contain personal information if you enter it there yourself.

The app uses local app storage; the browser version uses browser storage. The admin lock is an access barrier, not encryption of saved games. Device security, shared devices and operating-system backups, where applicable, are therefore relevant.

Network and Permissions

The current core game does not use its own advertising or analytics telemetry and does not require access to the microphone, camera, location or contacts. Saved games, game answers and learning history are not automatically transmitted to a Back2Who server. However, a connection may still be required for Apple purchases, installation or the initial loading of a browser version.

The App Store, TestFlight, the operating system and sharing services you choose may process data under their own rules, such as installation information, backups or voluntary TestFlight feedback. This processing is not the same as Back2Who's own analytics.

Optional Apple Purchases

Premium is handled through Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple processes payment and Apple account information; Back2Who does not receive credit card details. The app reads store product information, the transaction ID, verification status and expiry date to check entitlement. There is no dedicated Back2Who purchase server.

Previously verified entitlements are checked against Apple's local transaction records. The app keeps the verified status in memory until it expires; a network disruption does not extend it. A revocation may only be detected offline after Apple provides an update. Restoration, offer-code redemption and subscription management take place through Apple.

Further information: Apple's Privacy Policy.

Voluntary Exports and Support

A file or text is only created when you initiate an export or sharing action. On iPhone, you choose the recipient in the share sheet; in the browser, a file is downloaded. Nothing is sent automatically to the publisher.

Learning exports contain question feedback and the reduced context described above, not names or game answers. However, session IDs and time information mean they cannot be considered anonymous in all cases. Editorial exports may contain your free-text entries. Support feedback contains the text you enter and the app version. Check the contents before sharing and avoid confidential information about others.

Retention and Deletion

Local data remains stored until it is replaced or deliberately deleted; no automatic deletion period is configured. The most recently completed recap is replaced by the next one.

Under App Settings → Local Data, you can delete the saved game, last recap and question learning separately. These three actions do not affect app and game settings or editorial admin data. Names and optional information can be edited before the next game.

Uninstalling the app or deleting browser data may remove local data. This does not delete exports, external backups or copies held by recipients. Deleting local data does not cancel an Apple subscription; Apple's subscription management handles that.

This Website and GitHub Pages

This information page contains no JavaScript, trackers, embedded third-party fonts, contact form or cookies. It does not read app data and does not itself use local browser storage. External links are only accessed when clicked.

If the page is hosted on GitHub Pages in the future, GitHub receives the connection data required to retrieve it. According to GitHub, visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes, even without signing in. It would therefore be incorrect to state that no data is processed when visiting.

Details about processing, retention and possible international transfers are set out in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Source on IP logging: GitHub Pages documentation. The final hosting configuration has not yet been published.

Legal Bases and Your Rights

The applicable legal bases, responsibilities for local group use and hosting, and whether a data protection officer must be appointed need to be clarified before publication. Technical offline functionality is not, by itself, proof of compliance with data protection law.

Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies, rights include, in particular, access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, as well as, depending on the legal basis, the right to object or withdraw consent. You may lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.

The publisher cannot retrieve data stored exclusively locally from a server. Please do not send saved games or intimate information with an enquiry unless requested; any necessary identification of data should first take place through the privacy contact that is still to be added.

Support

The public support email address is 69bygone.soaking@icloud.com .

Under App Settings → Feedback / Support, you can create a short report and share it yourself. Helpful details include:

  • The app version and iPhone model, or the browser you are using.
  • What you did and what happened instead.
  • Whether the problem still occurs after restarting.

Please do not include Apple passwords, payment details, other people's real names or intimate answers. Only share screenshots without such information.

For subscription questions, you can select Restore Purchases or Manage Subscription in the app. For payment and refund enquiries, Apple provides support for subscriptions and purchases.
